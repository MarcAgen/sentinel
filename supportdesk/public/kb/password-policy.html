Password Policy

All TechCo employee accounts must comply with the following password requirements.

Requirements

  • Minimum 12 characters
  • At least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character
  • Cannot reuse the last 5 passwords
  • Must be changed every 90 days

Password manager

The IT department recommends using Bitwarden (self-hosted instance available at vault.techco.local) to manage your credentials securely.
