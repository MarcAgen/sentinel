VPN Access Guide

This guide explains how to connect to the TechCo corporate VPN using Cisco AnyConnect.

Requirements

  • Cisco AnyConnect client v4.10 or later
  • Valid Active Directory credentials
  • MFA enrolled on your mobile device

Connection steps

  1. Open Cisco AnyConnect and enter the gateway: vpn.techco.local
  2. Enter your domain username and password
  3. Approve the MFA push notification on your phone

If the connection fails with error 403, contact the helpdesk and reference this article.
